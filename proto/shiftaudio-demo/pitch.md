# ShiftAudio 5‑Minute Demo Script (Amazon Ops + WHS)

**Audience:** Operations + WHS leadership

**Goal:** Show how ShiftAudio improves safety compliance without disrupting throughput.

---

## 0:00–0:30 | Opening & Context
**Say:**
> “Thanks for the time. I’ll show a 5‑minute demo of ShiftAudio—an adaptive safety audio system that keeps teams informed without breaking flow. It blends safety messages with background audio and delivers at the right moments, especially in noisy environments.”

**Do:**
- Point to the **Shift Timeline** and **Clock** in the header.

---

## 0:30–1:30 | Start the Shift
**Say:**
> “I’ll start a shift so you can see the system in action. We can run in demo time or real time; I’ll keep it in demo so you can see it move quickly.”

**Do:**
- Set **Demo** in the time toggle.
- Click **Start Shift**.
- Highlight the **loading** state in Safety Announcements.

**Say:**
> “ShiftAudio builds a safety schedule based on cadence. It only shows the next three messages to keep the queue focused.”

---

## 1:30–2:30 | Cadence + Queue Behavior
**Say:**
> “Let’s adjust cadence. Low is 20 minutes, standard is 10, high is 5. This can be tuned by your WHS policy.”

**Do:**
- Change the **Cadence** selector.
- Click **Refresh** in the queue to regenerate the schedule.

**Say:**
> “The queue updates immediately, and the ‘Now’ card always shows what’s coming next.”

---

## 2:30–3:30 | Delivery Logic (Bed vs No Bed)
**Say:**
> “If bed music is playing, ShiftAudio waits for a crossfade to insert the message so it never collides with critical audio.”

**Do:**
- Start bed audio in **Background Audio**.
- Click **Crossfade Now** to demonstrate delivery on crossfade.

**Say:**
> “If there’s no bed music, messages deliver exactly at the scheduled time. That keeps safety consistent in quiet zones.”

**Do:**
- Pause bed audio and let a scheduled message trigger.

---

## 3:30–4:15 | Breaks + Compliance
**Say:**
> “ShiftAudio also respects breaks. Break start and end announcements are explicit, and non‑emergency messages pause during breaks.”

**Do:**
- Show the **Break window** in the Shift Timeline.
- Point to the “Break starting/ending” audio files if asked.

---

## 4:15–4:45 | Emergency Override
**Say:**
> “In emergencies, safety overrides everything. All other messages stop immediately, and the bed audio pauses.”

**Do:**
- Trigger **Emergency Override** and then end it.

**Say:**
> “Once emergency ends, the system resumes only if audio was already running.”

---

## 4:45–5:00 | Close / Value Summary
**Say:**
> “ShiftAudio increases safety compliance by delivering messages at the right moments without disrupting flow. Operations keep throughput steady, WHS gets consistent delivery, and teams receive clear guidance in noisy environments.”

**Ask:**
> “Would you like a pilot in a single site to measure compliance lift and operator feedback?”

---

## Optional Q&A Prompts
- “We can tune cadence per site or per shift.”
- “Messages can map to specific zones in production.”
- “We support emergency overrides and audit logging for compliance.”
